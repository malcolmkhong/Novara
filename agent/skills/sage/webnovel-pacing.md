# Skill: Webnovel Pacing (Sage)

Diagnostic guides, not blind quotas — the brief wins conflicts.

## Chapter skeleton
- Open in a concrete scene on the previous hook — no recap.
- A turn every 800-1400 characters: new info, reversal, cost, or a promise
  planted/paid.
- End on the brief's closing feel: open question, threat, or decision.

## Density & payoff
- Every ~1000 characters advances something real. Scenery, repeated inner
  monologue, and small talk advance nothing — cut or add a beat.
- Each chapter delivers one felt step of progress (status / resource /
  relationship / risk). Transition chapters keep one small beat.
- Pressure builds slow, releases fast, pauses, escalates again.

## Genre tilt
- 玄幻/修仙：动作与结果承担叙事。言情：关系位移可见。悬疑/怪谈：线索可回收，恐惧来自规则。都市/电竞：反馈—反应—反制。
